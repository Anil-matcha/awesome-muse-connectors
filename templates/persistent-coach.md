# Persistent Coach

**Category:** Personal workflow<br>
**Integrations:** Muse<br>
**Approval level:** Low to medium — installing a powerup only changes your Muse workspace; paid packs ($19 one-time) require your approval before purchase.

## Use it for

Turning Muse from a generic assistant into a persistent coach that remembers you — training logs, prayer journals, nutrition history — instead of starting over every conversation.

## Prompt

> Install the [PACK NAME] powerup from poweruppacks.com. When asked, I will give you my license key, then answer the onboarding questions. After the install, confirm what was set up: which track I am on, what gets logged, and when you will check in with me.
>
> Replace [PACK NAME] with one of: marathon coach, strength coach, hybrid engine, meal prep pro, macro chef, macro tracker, prayer keeper, bible study coach, daily devotional.
>
> Paid packs are $19 one-time at poweruppacks.com. The free Daily Devotional Lite pack needs only an email claim.

## First supervised run

Try the free Daily Devotional Lite pack first — say "install the daily devotional lite powerup from poweruppacks.com". Verify the assistant asks onboarding questions and references your saved state in a later session before buying a paid pack.
